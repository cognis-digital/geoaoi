geoaoi is a small command-line tool that works with lists of GPS coordinates. You give it a CSV file of locations and it can draw a bounding box around them, check which ones fall inside a defined area (like a fence around a building or a city block), and compare two snapshots of location data to see what moved, arrived, or left. It is useful for anyone tracking assets, vehicles, or points of interest who needs quick answers from coordinate logs without setting up a mapping service or database.
